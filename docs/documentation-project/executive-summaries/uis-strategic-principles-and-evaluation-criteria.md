## Part 1: Strategic Vision

**Strategic Vision**– What Success Looks Like

**Mission**: Create a single, unified knowledge ecosystem serving as the "Single Source of Truth" for all MagTek technical information. **System Characteristics:**

* Non-technical experts (Support, Marketing, PMs) can contribute easily

* Ready for AI integration
* Scalable for future product lines

## Part 3: Evaluation Criteria

**Evaluation Criteria**– How We Assess Platform Solutions

### The "Low Tech User" Test (Refined from discussion)

_"A tool that is perfect for engineers but unusable for marketing and support fails the test. We are not just choosing a documentation tool; we are choosing a**collaboration platform**that must serve the entire MagTek ecosystem.“_

### The "Low Tech User" Standard

* Contributors will receive appropriate training
* Contributors should have basic professional aptitude
* Not everyone will contribute to all documentation (realistic expectation)
* System itself must be accessible and usable by non-technical professionals
* Actual contributions and changes to documents will be approved and vetted by SMEs.
* Ability to approve doc changes will be limited to specific personnel.
* **Test**: Can Marketing/Support make doc update suggestions independently after training, without ongoing engineering support?

**Why?**

* The UIS must serve people across all departments to break down information silos and end "tribal knowledge" culture.
* We cannot solve information fragmentation if only engineers can contribute.

**Questions for the group**: Is this the right standard and the right criteria? Should we optimize for technically-minded users only?

## Appendix A: Strategic Objectives

**Strategic Objectives**– The Problems We're Solving

### 🎯 Eliminate Information Silos

* Dismantle fragmentation across Agile, Jira, Email, Support site, and local drives
* Dismantle “tribal knowledge” culture
* Create single source of truth

### 🎯 Accelerate Engineering Velocity

* Integrate documentation into development lifecycle
* Remove friction of updating legacy Word manual

### 🎯 Reduce "Time-to-Information"

* Cut time internal teams spend hunting for answers
* Reduce "interrupt cost" on Engineering, Support, and other departments

### 🎯 Deflect Support Tickets

* Empower customers with searchable, self-service portal
* Provide actionable troubleshooting guides and API references

### 🎯 Ensure Regulatory Compliance

* Leverage Git-based version control for immutable audit trail
* Support PCI PTS and PCI DSS requirements (PCI Delta evaluation)

**Question for the group**: Are these the right problems to solve?

**Primary Evaluation Criteria**– Essential for Success

**Ease of Use & Contributor Experience**

* Usable by both technical (engineers) AND non-technical (Marketing, Support, PMs) contributors
* If it's not easy, adoption will fail
* _Directly addresses the "tribal knowledge" problem_

**Built-in Editorial & Collaboration Tools**

* Inline comments, suggestions, clear review/approval workflow
* No reliance on complex Git commands for non-technical users
* _Replaces inefficient email/chat review cycles_

**Integration with GitHub Workflow**

* Must integrate with GitHub backend
* Fits engineering team's existing process
* _Strategic mandate from leadership_

[Full details in Evaluation Criteria document with secondary/tertiary criteria on GitBook collaborative site](https://magtek.gitbook.io/magtek-pilot-gitbooks/docs/documentation-project/unified-information-system-platform-evaluation-criteria-definition)

**Primary Evaluation Criteria**– Essential for Success

**Operational Sustainability**

* The platform must be manageable by the current technical writing team of one • Should not require programming, DevOps, or system administration expertise.
* _This is a critical constraint_

**Permissioning & Access Control**

* Secure management of internal (proprietary) and external (customer) content
* Single unified system with different visibility levels
* _Core requirement for unified system_

**Compliance & Audit Trail**

* Ability to track all changes, see who made what edits and when, and roll back to previous versions if needed
* Essential for accountability, audit trails, and recovering from errors
* _Directly supports PCI PTS and PCI DSS requirements_
