**📡 Contactless/NFC Required**

This feature requires contactless (NFC) card reading capability.

**Compatible Devices:**

* DynaFlex (with Contactless)
* DynaProx (with Contactless)
* DynaFlex II Go (with Contactless)

**Supported Technologies:**

* EMV Contactless
* NFC/MIFARE
* Apple VAS
* Google Smart Tap
