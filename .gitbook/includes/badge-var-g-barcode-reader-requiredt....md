**📷 Barcode Reader Required**

This feature requires a device with integrated barcode scanning capability.

**Compatible Devices:**

* DynaFlex (with BCR)
* DynaProx (with BCR)
* Models with integrated camera/barcode scanner

**Supported Formats:** See [Barcode Symbologies](/broken/pages/f2ab84769b1a1aa81f3c35b3035eedd0ce8e0a2f).
